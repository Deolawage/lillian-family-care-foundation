Our Story

From a Small Idea to a Nationwide Movement

Lillian Family Care Foundation was founded in 2024 by a group of passionate Nigerians who believed that proximity to opportunity should not determine a person's destiny. What started as a small community outreach in Ondo has grown into a structured, impactful NGO serving communities across multiple states.


Over the years, we have touched thousands of lives through targeted programs in education, healthcare, and youth empowerment — building bridges between underserved communities and the resources they deserve.

Our Mission

To empower underserved Nigerian communities by providing sustainable access to quality education, healthcare, and economic opportunities — ensuring that no individual is left behind regardless of their background or location.

Our Vision

A Nigeria where every citizen has equal access to the tools, resources, and opportunities needed to live a dignified, fulfilling, and prosperous life — regardless of where they were born.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

01

Integrity

We operate with full transparency in all our programs and financial dealings. Every naira donated is accounted for and reported openly.

02

Inclusion

No community is too remote, no individual too forgotten to benefit from our work. We go where others won't.

03

Impact

We measure success by the lives changed, not the programs launched. Every initiative is outcome-driven and community-led.

04

Innovation

We deploy creative, scalable solutions to solve age-old community problems — leveraging technology, partnerships, and grassroots knowledge.

05

Compassion

Every beneficiary is treated with dignity and respect. We lead with empathy in everything we do — from program design to field delivery.

06

Collaboration

We believe in the power of partnerships — with government bodies, corporate sponsors, and local community leaders to multiply our impact.

What We Do

Our Programs

Education for All

We provide scholarships, school supplies, and digital learning tools to over 3,000 children in underserved communities. Our education program also trains teachers in modern pedagogy and builds reading corners in rural schools.

3,200+ Children Enrolled
12 Schools Supported
Lagos, Kano, Enugu States

Community Health Drive

Our mobile medical units travel to remote communities providing free consultations, medications, maternal care, and health education. We partner with licensed medical professionals who volunteer their time and expertise.

5,000+ Patients Treated
20 Outreaches Per Year
Free Medications Provided

Youth Empowerment Hub

We run vocational training, entrepreneurship bootcamps, and provide micro-grant funding for young Nigerians aged 18–35. Our graduates have gone on to start businesses, secure employment, and become community leaders themselves.

800+ Youth Trained
₦12M+ in Micro-Grants
68% Employment Rate
