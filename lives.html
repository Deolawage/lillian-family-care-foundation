Education

From a Mud-Floored Classroom to the University of Lagos

When Chiamaka was 11 years old, she walked 6 kilometres to school every day in Enugu — that is, until Lillian Family Care Foundation arrived in her community. Today, at 19, she is a first-year Medical student, fully funded by our scholarship program. "They didn't just pay my fees," she says. "They believed in me when no one else would."

March 2024 📍 Enugu State
Healthcare

A Mother's Life Saved by Our Mobile Clinic

Hauwa Abdullahi, 34, was in critical condition during a complicated pregnancy. Our mobile clinic was the only medical presence within 40km of her village. Our team stabilised her and facilitated her transfer to a hospital. Both mother and child survived.

January 2024 📍 Kano State
Empowerment

How a ₦150,000 Micro-Grant Became a Thriving Business

Emeka was 24 and unemployed when he attended our entrepreneurship bootcamp in Lagos. With a micro-grant of ₦150,000 and the skills he gained, he launched a mobile phone repair business. Two years later, he employs three other young men from his street.

Community

Building a Reading Corner in Badagry

In partnership with local volunteers, Lillian Family Care Foundation set up a fully stocked reading corner at a primary school in Badagry. Over 400 pupils now have access to books for the first time. The school's literacy rate improved by 40% within one academic session.

September 2023 📍 Lagos State
Healthcare

Free Eye Clinic Restores Sight to 80 Residents

During our annual health outreach in Ogun State, we partnered with a team of ophthalmologists to conduct free eye examinations. 80 residents received free prescription glasses; 12 were referred for cataract surgery fully funded by Lillian Family Care Foundation.

July 2023 📍 Ogun State
Education

Digital Literacy Program Reaches 500 Students

We launched a digital literacy initiative equipping 500 secondary school students with basic computer skills. With donated laptops and volunteer tutors, students learned to type, use productivity software, and safely navigate the internet — skills that open doors to the modern economy.
